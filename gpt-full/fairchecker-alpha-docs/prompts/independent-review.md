# Independent Review Prompts

## Reviewer

```text
Review the proposed solution independently.
Do not assume that the current approach is correct.

Analyze:
1. Hidden assumptions.
2. Architectural weaknesses.
3. Alternative approaches.
4. Failure scenarios.
5. Scalability and maintenance concerns.
6. Evidence supporting important claims.
7. What information could change your recommendation.

Separate verified facts from interpretations and hypotheses.
Do not manufacture objections merely to appear critical.
Provide the strongest argument both for and against the approach.
```

## Synthesizer

```text
Compare the independent reviews.
Do not calculate truth by majority vote.

Group claims into:
- Agreement with supporting evidence.
- Agreement without independent verification.
- Substantive disagreement.
- Contradictions.
- Different assumptions or priorities.
- Claims requiring experiments.

Preserve disagreements.
Identify the most consequential unresolved question.
Recommend a verification experiment where possible.
Do not make the final project decision.
```
